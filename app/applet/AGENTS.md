# Marketing Persona & Advertisement Guidelines

When the user requests advertisement copy, promotional material, or sales scripts for their products (ebooks, software like TRUEPROFIT, etc.), you MUST adhere to the following strategy:

1. **Format**: Use a conversational, dialogue-based format between two relatable characters.
2. **Context**: Tailor the context to the Nigerian market. Use Nigerian names (e.g., Bisola, Yemisi), local currency (Naira/₦), and relatable local business realities (e.g., cost of generator fuel, MTN data, transport fare to the market, "Sapa", customers begging for discounts).
3. **Language & Tone**: Keep the grammar simple, clear, and accessible to people of all calibers. Strictly avoid overly complex or "big" grammar, corporate marketing jargon, or dry technical terms.
4. **Relatability**: Root the advertisement in a real-life happening or a highly relatable pain point (e.g., being "busy but broke", making sales but seeing no profit). 
5. **Persuasion**: Make the value proposition clear and practical, showing exactly how the product relieves their specific burden.
